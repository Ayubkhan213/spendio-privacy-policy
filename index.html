Privacy Policy

Effective: October 13, 2026 Developer: A&K Tech

Spendio is a personal finance app for tracking expenses, budgets, debts and shared money pools. It is built offline‑first — it works the moment you install it, with no account required — and offers an optional free account for people who want backup, multi‑device sync, or to track a shared debt or pool with someone else. This policy explains exactly what that means for your data.

1Overview

This policy covers the Spendio mobile app, published by A&K Tech ("we", "us"). It describes what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have — in plain language, without legal filler.

The short version: everything you track stays on your device by default. A few features that only make sense when another person is involved — shared debts and money pools — need an account and a server to keep both sides in sync, and those are entirely optional.

2Data we collect

What Spendio stores falls into three clearly separated groups.

Always on your device only

  • Budgets & category limits
  • Recurring transactions & subscriptions
  • Tasks & reminders
  • Theme, language & currency settings
  • Local profile name/photo, if you skip account creation

Only if you create an account

  • Your email address
  • Your password — stored as a salted, one‑way hash; we cannot read it
  • A security question & answer, for account recovery only
  • Your phone number — optional, only needed if you want to be found when someone invites you to a Money Pool

Only for features shared with another person

  • Transactions you add: amount, category, note, date, optional receipt photo
  • Debts you link to someone: their name, amount, due date, optional photo, repayment history
  • Money Pools: members, deposits, expenses and how they're split

Data in the third group is stored on our server only so it can correctly appear on both your device and the other participant's device — it is never used for advertising, never sold, and never analyzed for any purpose beyond making that feature work.

3Guest mode — fully offline, no account

You can use Spendio's core features — expenses, budgets, recurring bills, analytics, tasks — without ever signing up. In this mode, nothing you enter ever leaves your device: no server call, no account, no data collection of any kind. Creating an account later is always your choice, never a requirement.

4How we use data

  • To run the app's core features entirely on your device.
  • To sync your transactions, debts and money pools across your own devices, if you're signed in.
  • To let a debt or a money pool you share with someone else show correctly on both of your devices.
  • To verify your identity and let you recover your account if you forget your password.
  • To generate local reminders for budgets and recurring bills — these are created entirely on your device and never sent through our server.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside the features above.

5Storage & security

  • All data sent to our server travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — only a salted, one‑way hash.
  • Your session token is stored in your device's secure, OS‑level storage, not in a plain settings file.
  • Receipt and photo attachments are compressed before upload to keep transfers small and fast.
  • You can lock the app itself with a local PIN or app‑lock, independent of your account password.

6Sharing with others

We don't run ads, embed ad SDKs, use marketing trackers, or sell your data to anyone. The only "sharing" that happens is the kind you ask for directly: when you link a debt to another Spendio user or invite someone into a money pool, that specific person sees only the information that feature needs them to see — nothing else about you or your other data. Inviting someone to a Money Pool uses their phone number to find their account; we don't access your phone's contacts to do this.

7App permissions

8Your rights & control

  • Edit or delete any transaction, debt, budget, or other entry at any time, directly in the app.
  • Permanently delete your account from Profile → Settings — this removes your synced transactions, debts and money pool data from our server.
  • Clear the app's local data or uninstall it to instantly remove everything stored only on your device.
  • Even without reinstalling the app, email us to request deletion of your account and its data — we complete these requests within 30 days.
Deleting your account removes your server‑side data. Local‑only data (budgets, recurring bills, tasks) was never on our server in the first place and is removed the moment you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

9Children's privacy

Spendio is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for any meaningful change, note it here. Continuing to use Spendio after an update means you accept the revised policy.
